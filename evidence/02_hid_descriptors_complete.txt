    FATAL ROOT CAUSE FOR KINDLE INCOMPATIBILITY:
    IINE_reader uses Usage Page 0x0C (Consumer Control) and emits Volume Up (bit 6) and Volume Down (bit 7).
    Kindle OS input_manager capability_filter strictly drops all Volume keys.
    Kindle REQUIRES standard Keyboard Report (Usage Page 0x07) with KEY_PAGEUP (0x4B) and KEY_PAGEDOWN (0x4E).
